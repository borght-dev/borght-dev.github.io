  ### What helps in a first message

  A few lines is enough: what the system does, what's stuck or slowing you down, roughly what timeline you have in mind. I'll usually reply within two working days. If it's not a fit, I'll say so and point you in a better direction where I can.

Good reasons to get in touch:

- A **legacy .NET application** that needs to be modernized and you want an evidence-based plan before committing budget. See the [.NET Modernization Assessment](/#assessment).
- A **technical project that keeps getting postponed** because your team doesn't have the capacity.
- An **architecture or delivery question** you'd like a senior second opinion on.
- Getting **practical engineering leverage from AI tooling** for your development team.
- An exploratory conversation about whether a focused engagement makes sense.
